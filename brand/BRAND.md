# Taki brand

## The idea

Taki is Japanese for waterfall: many streams falling into one place. The mark is four streams that start apart and curve together into one, which falls into a pool and ripples once. Many agents, one window. The palette is sumi ink, washi paper and the vermilion of a torii gate.
